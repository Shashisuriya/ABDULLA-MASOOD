Your enquiry & privacy

When you send a quotation enquiry, you share your name, email address, optional phone number, project description and any attached drawings with Abdullah Masood Steel Works & Welding Workshop L.L.C. so the team can assess your requirements and contact you.

How enquiries are delivered

The form is processed by FormSubmit and delivered to alimoosakhan@gmail.com. FormSubmit provides spam protection and may ask you to complete a verification step. Its documentation states that form submissions are retained for 30 days; uploaded files are not retained in its submission archive. Please review FormSubmit’s privacy policy for its handling of data.

Only attach information needed for the quotation. For sensitive project drawings, contact the team first to agree a suitable sharing method.

WhatsApp and external links

WhatsApp enquiries are handled through WhatsApp under its own privacy terms. Google Maps and the hosting provider may process technical information when you use their services. This website does not add advertising trackers or analytics scripts.

Questions or deletion requests

Contact alimoosakhan@gmail.com or +971 55 779 8855 to ask about your enquiry or request deletion of information held by the business.
